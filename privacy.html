Doseline

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Doseline doesn't collect any data. Everything you enter (your medications, schedules and the doses you log) is stored only on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

  • Medication names, strengths, instructions and schedules you type in.
  • The times you log doses.
  • Your reminder settings.

This stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those turned on, under Apple's privacy terms. We have no access to it.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No internet connection is used at all.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders and alarms are scheduled locally on your iPhone using iOS notifications and, if you turn it on, iOS alarms. They are not sent through any server. You can turn them off at any time in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or delete the app. Either removes everything it stored.

Children

Doseline doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
