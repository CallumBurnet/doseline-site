Doseline

Support

Doseline reminds you to take your medication. For "every few hours" medications, the next dose counts from when you actually took the last one, so a late dose pushes the rest back.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at github.com/CallumBurnet/doseline-site/issues and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

I'm not getting reminders

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Doseline and make sure notifications are allowed. Notification sounds follow the ring/silent switch. For something that rings even on silent, turn on Ring as an alarm for that medication (iOS 26 or later).

How do the "every few hours" reminders work?

When you log a dose, the next one is scheduled that many hours later. If you set an Only between window (for example 6am–2pm), nothing is scheduled outside it; a dose that would land after the window moves to the start of the next day's window.

I logged a dose by mistake

Tap Undo straight after logging, or open the medication (or the History tab) and delete that dose. The schedule recalculates on its own.

I took it earlier and forgot to log it

Open the medication and tap I took it at a different time… to log the real time.

How do I stop the repeat reminders?

Log the dose (in the app, or Took it on the notification), or tap Snooze. You can change how often and how many times they repeat in Settings.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Doseline doesn't give medical advice. Always follow your doctor, pharmacist or the medicine's label.
